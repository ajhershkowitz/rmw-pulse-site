Privacy policy: RMW Social Pulse

RMW Social Pulse is a private dashboard used only by the owner of the accounts it reads, Rabbi Mark Wildes of the Manhattan Jewish Experience (MJE): rabbinic figure and Jewish activist. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner and the people they share the password with.

  • It reads only the owner's own accounts, through each platform's official API and with the owner's permission: public numbers about the owner's posts and followers, and comments and messages sent to the owner's accounts.
  • Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, reads new comments and messages to pick the ones worth the owner's attention. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • The data is stored in the owner's private GitHub repository and shown only on a password-protected page. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • A comment deleted on its platform is dropped on the next update. Picked messages are kept for at most 12 months.
  • To have anything removed, contact the account owner through the account itself.
